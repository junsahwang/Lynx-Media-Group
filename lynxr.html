lynxr, a script tool from lynxr LLC

Lynx Media Group · By the Lynx Media Group team · Updated September 15, 2026

lynxr is a script tool for short-form creators, made by our partner company, lynxr LLC. lynxr LLC and Lynx Media Group are partner companies doing two halves of one job: Lynx Media Group runs short-form video campaigns for brands, with creators, and lynxr LLC makes lynxr for the creator's side of that work — writing the script. Creators can use lynxr directly at lynxr.io: paste a public TikTok or Instagram link, and it rewrites that video's format as a script for the brand they make content for.

Who makes lynxr?

lynxr is made by lynxr LLC, a separate partner company of Lynx Media Group LLC, the short-form video agency in Allston, Massachusetts founded by Gawin Hsu and Junsa Hwang. lynxr was created by Lynx Media Group co-founder and CTO Junsa Hwang. Both founders' backgrounds are in our team section.

How does lynxr fit with Lynx Media Group's campaigns?

lynxr is the format intelligence Junsa built, and it now powers Lynx Media Group's own campaign strategy as well as the tool creators use directly at lynxr.io. How it works walks through the whole engine, from creator matching to weekly iteration.

What do creators get at lynxr.io?

The public version of lynxr is built for creators who make short-form video for brands, and for the people who brief them. You paste a public TikTok or Instagram link; lynxr watches the video, transcribes what is said, works out the format underneath, and rewrites that format as a script for the company you are making content for.

  • There is no file upload — a public video link is the only input.
  • One link can become one script per brand you make content for.
  • It never asks for a platform password and never posts anything on your behalf.
  • It is free to start; current plans are on the lynxr pricing page.

Try lynxr at lynxr.io, or read lynxr's own questions and answers.

Creators who want a script from a video that already worked use lynxr on their own at lynxr.io, made by our partner company lynxr LLC. Frequently asked questions

Is lynxr part of Lynx Media Group?

No. lynxr is made by lynxr LLC, a separate partner company of Lynx Media Group LLC, the short-form video agency in Allston, Massachusetts founded by Gawin Hsu and Junsa Hwang. lynxr was created by co-founder and CTO Junsa Hwang, and it now powers Lynx Media Group's own campaign strategy as well.

How are lynxr and Lynx Media Group related?

lynxr LLC, the company that makes lynxr, and Lynx Media Group are partner companies doing two halves of one job. Lynx Media Group runs short-form video campaigns for brands, with creators. lynxr LLC makes lynxr for the creator's side of that work: writing the script.

What does lynxr do?

lynxr turns a short-form video into a script. You send it a public link to a TikTok or Instagram video; it watches the video, transcribes what is said, works out the format underneath — the hook, the beats, the order they come in — and rewrites that format as a script for the company you are making content for.

Who is lynxr for?

lynxr.io is built for creators who make short-form video for brands, and for the people who brief them. Brands that want creators, format research, publishing, and reporting handled for them work with Lynx Media Group directly.

Is lynxr free?

lynxr is free to start. Current plans and limits are listed on the lynxr pricing page at lynxr.io/pricing.

Is lynxr related to the Honeywell LYNXR alarm panel?

No. lynxr (lynxr.io) is a short-form video tool made by lynxr LLC, a partner company of Lynx Media Group. It has no connection to Honeywell's LYNXR security control panels or to the Lynx R mixed reality headsets, which share a similar name.
