How to become a UGC creator

UGC creators get paid to make short-form videos for brands — without needing a big following, a studio, or an agent. Here's what the work actually looks like and how to get into it.

What the job is

You script, film, and edit short videos around a brand's product: hooks, demos, storytimes, POVs. The brand gets platform-native content; you get paid per video, per campaign, or on retainer. The formats are covered in our UGC explainer.

The skills that matter

  • Hooks — the first two seconds decide everything (what a hook is)
  • Pacing — native rhythm, cuts, and on-screen text that hold attention
  • Trend awareness — sounds and formats adapted to the brand, not copied
  • Consistency — producing multiple videos a week without going stale

You don't need followers

Brands pay for content that performs, and platforms distribute videos on merit. One Lynx client reached 4.29 million views from an account with 2,672 followers — the content did the work. Understand why in how short-form reach works.

Build a small, sharp portfolio

Three to five strong examples in your niche beat thirty generic ones. Show a hook, a demo, and a story — the range a brand campaign actually needs.

Getting paid, and getting steady work

Solo creators cold-pitch brands one at a time. A creator community flips that: campaigns come to you, matched to your niche, with reliable payment terms (how creators get paid). That's what Lynx runs — apply through the creator application and show us your platforms and style.
