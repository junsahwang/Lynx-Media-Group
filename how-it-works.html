Step 01

Your brand has something people would talk about

Every product has a niche that already scrolls TikTok and Reels daily — students, nurses, founders, foodies. The problem is never the audience. It's that polished ads get skipped and your brand isn't in the conversation.

Step 02

We match you with creators from your niche

Not rented influencers — a dedicated team from our creator community who already speak your audience's language. They learn your product and keep creative ownership of their style, because that's what keeps it authentic.

Step 03

They publish platform-native videos — daily

Hooks, demos, storytimes, POVs. Volume matters because short-form reach is probabilistic: a minority of posts drive the majority of views, so every post is another ticket. For Medceptor, that meant 1,200 posts in a single month.

Step 04

The algorithm auditions every single video

Each post is shown to a fresh test audience — followers not required. Strong watch time earns a bigger audience, then a bigger one. Weak videos quietly stop; nothing is damaged. The upside is uncapped, the downside is a shrug.
