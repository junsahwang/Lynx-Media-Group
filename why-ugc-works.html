The data

Why UGC works: the numbers vs traditional ads

"UGC outperforms ads" gets said a lot. Here's what the numbers behind that claim actually look like — industry benchmarks first, our own campaign data second, and the mechanism that explains both.

The trust gap is the foundation

Industry surveys consistently find that around 90% of consumers say they trust user-generated content more than traditional advertising, and roughly 84% report trusting brands more when they feature real people and UGC. Ads start every interaction with a credibility handicap; UGC starts with the benefit of the doubt.

Engagement: people watch what feels human

UGC-style ad creative earns roughly 73% higher engagement than studio-produced ads in platform studies, and UGC broadly outperforms brand-produced content on engagement across social networks. This matches what the feed mechanics predict: watch time drives distribution, and viewers give people more watch time than they give commercials — the mechanism we break down in how short-form reach works.

Conversion: attention that actually buys

  • Shoppers who interact with UGC convert at roughly double the rate (studies report a ~104% conversion lift)
  • Product pages featuring UGC and reviews report conversion increases of 150–270% depending on category
  • Our own tracked data agrees: creator posts for Memo produced a 3.49% conversion rate, and one 30-day Medceptor campaign ran alongside a +21% revenue increase — full numbers in our case studies

Cost: the same attention for less

Campaigns running UGC creative report around 50% lower cost per click than campaigns running polished production assets, and brands commissioning creator content instead of studio shoots cut content production costs by as much as 70%. The math compounds: cheaper content, cheaper clicks, and organic distribution that paid media never gets for free.

Why the numbers come out this way

None of this is magic — it's selection pressure. Feeds rank by watch time, viewers reward authenticity, and platforms redistribute whatever wins. Traditional ads are built for interruption; UGC is built for the feed it lives in. The format that matches the environment wins the environment's metrics, and the metrics above are just that fact measured five different ways.

Sources

Industry figures above are drawn from published UGC statistics roundups, including Billo, inBeat Agency, Taggbox, and Archive. Ranges vary by study and category; Lynx campaign figures are our own tracked data.
