UGC campaign management, explained

Running one creator is easy. Running ten creators posting daily, on strategy, with the data feeding back into next week's content — that's a job. This page explains what a UGC campaign manager actually does, and when it makes sense to hand it to an agency.

What campaign management includes

  • Creator sourcing and matching — finding creators whose audience and style fit your niche, vetting their past output, and onboarding them to your product
  • Content strategy — hooks, formats, and angles planned against what the data says works, not guesses
  • Posting calendars — consistent daily volume across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, because reach is probabilistic and consistency is the strategy
  • Weekly iteration — reading performance, doubling down on winners, retiring what flopped
  • Reporting — views, engagement, conversion, and revenue impact in one place, so you know what you're paying for

In-house vs agency

Managing creators in-house means recruiting them, negotiating rates, chasing deliverables, tracking performance, and replacing dropouts — typically a full-time role before a single video improves. An agency amortizes that operation across clients: you get the managed creator team, the calendar, and the reporting without hiring for it. We compare the options honestly in agency vs freelancers.

What good management looks like in numbers

Managed volume compounds: our Medceptor campaign ran 10 creators publishing 1,200 posts in 30 days — 4.1 million views and a +38% revenue increase alongside it. Reach without audience works too: 145 managed posts took Memo to 4.29 million views from 2,672 followers. Both are in our case studies.

What to ask any campaign manager

  • How many creators, and how are they matched to my niche?
  • What posting volume is committed per week?
  • How do learnings from week one change week three?
  • What exactly appears in the report, and how often?

If the answers are vague, the "management" is a group chat. Ours is a system — see how it works step by step, or book an intro call.
